## BioManager 1.9.0

### New

- **A Breeding cage can have more than one litter at a time.** When a trio's second female gives birth before the first litter is weaned, **Litter born** asks **Another litter** (both are kept) or **Correct the date**, and takes the pups and the mother. Each litter has its own weaning and genotyping days on **Home**, in the calendar and in the reminder emails, and **Genotyping** and **Wean** ask which litter, the oldest first.
  - **一个繁殖笼可以同时有不止一窝。** 三只鼠合笼时，第二只母鼠在第一窝断奶前产仔，**产仔** 会问是 **另一窝**（两窝都保留）还是 **更正日期**，并记录仔数和母鼠。每一窝在 **首页**、日历和提醒邮件里都有自己的断奶日和基因型鉴定日，**基因型鉴定** 和 **断奶** 会问是哪一窝，最早的排在前面。
- **Animals that may need a look**, a card on **Home**: mice that are losing weight, notes and weights that use a welfare word, mice not weighed when their experiment measures body weight, and mice whose status and experiment disagree. It says what the records show, never a diagnosis. Set how much weight loss to flag in **Settings → General → Weight loss to flag** (15% unless your lab chooses another). Animal care sees the lab's; everyone else sees their own, with **Show the lab’s** beside it. A notice comes once a day, and AI assistants see the same checks.
  - **首页** 新增 **可能需要查看的动物** 卡片：体重在下降的小鼠、备注或体重记录里出现福利相关词语的动物、实验测体重却没按时称重的小鼠，以及状态和实验对不上的小鼠。它只说记录里有什么，不做诊断。在 **设置 → 一般 → 提示的体重下降** 设定下降多少才提示（默认 15%，实验室可以改）。动物管理人员看到全实验室的，其他人看到自己的，旁边有 **查看全实验室的**。每天提醒一次，AI 助手也能看到同样的检查。

### Fixed

- **Uploaded files open only for people who may see them.** A picture on a private notebook page or a capture's photo used to open for anyone signed in who had its address. Now a file opens for someone who may see a record it belongs to; a file no record uses opens only for whoever uploaded it and the admins.
  - **上传的文件只对有权查看的人开放。** 以前，私密笔记页里的图片或采集的照片，任何已登录且知道地址的人都能打开。现在只有能看到它所属记录的人才能打开；没有记录使用的文件，只有上传者和管理员能打开。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.9.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  The database upgrades itself on start: it gains each cage's litters (a cage's birth date becomes its first litter) and a record of what each uploaded file belongs to, filled in from the records that use it. Nothing already there changes, and every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).

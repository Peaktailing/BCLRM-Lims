## BioManager 1.15.0

### New

- **Stimulation in the regimen.** A manipulation can be a **Stimulation** (optogenetic, electrical, TMS…), for mice, zebrafish and your own organisms, with its **Frequency**, **Intensity**, **Pulse width** and **Duration** as you write them (*20 Hz*, *5 mW*, *5 ms*, *30 min*). They show in the regimen, in the day's name on the calendar, in saved regimens, in the plan kept at **Start** and in **Export**, and an AI assistant can plan them.
  - **处理方案里的刺激。** 操作可以是 **刺激** （光遗传、电刺激、TMS 等），适用于小鼠、斑马鱼和你自己的物种，按你的写法记下 **频率**、**强度**、**脉宽** 和 **时长** （*20 Hz*、*5 mW*、*5 ms*、*30 min*）。它们显示在处理方案里、日历上那一天的名称里、保存的方案里、**开始** 时记下的计划里和 **导出** 里，AI 助手也可以安排。
- **New experiment offers readouts worked out from Cts.** Beside the measured readouts, the **Readout** in **New experiment** lists ΔCt, ΔΔCt and fold change, asking for the **Control group** for the last two; the mouse colony's form now has its **Readout** too.
  - **新建实验可以选由 Ct 算出的观测指标。** 除了直接测量的观测指标，**新建实验** 的 **观测指标** 里还有 ΔCt、ΔΔCt 和倍数变化，后两者会要求填写 **对照组**；小鼠群体的表单现在也有 **观测指标**。

### Works differently

- **A measure's kind can be changed.** The **•••** beside a measure has its **Kind**: a number, ΔCt, ΔΔCt or fold change. Its readings are worked out again as the new kind: a number keeps each value, ΔCt takes each from its two Cts, and ΔΔCt or fold change needs the two Cts of every reading (a number or a typed ΔCt has none, so it says how many and changes nothing). Each change is one step in **Batch history**, which **Undo** takes back.
  - **指标的类型可以修改。** 指标旁边的 **•••** 里有 **类型**：数值、ΔCt、ΔΔCt 或倍数变化。读数会按新类型重新计算：数值保留原值，ΔCt 由每个读数的两个 Ct 算出，ΔΔCt 或倍数变化需要每个读数都有两个 Ct（数值或直接填的 ΔCt 没有，这时会说明有多少个，不做任何改动）。每次修改在 **批量操作记录** 里是一步，可以 **撤销**。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.15.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version adds four columns to a table (a stimulation's settings); updating adds them, and keeps everything else as it was. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).

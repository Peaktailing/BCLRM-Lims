## BioManager 1.8.3

### Works differently

- **The calendar follows light and dark as you switch.** Its cells, weekend shading and event colours now come from the app's own colours, so changing **Settings → Appearance & language** (or the computer's light or dark) recolours the calendar at once, without reloading.
  - **日历会随浅色和深色模式立即切换。** 日历格子、周末底色和事件颜色现在都来自应用自己的配色，修改 **设置 → 外观与语言**（或电脑的浅色/深色）后，日历会马上换色，不用刷新。

### Website

- The demo clips on the website play in the page's language (Chinese on the Chinese site) and follow the site's light or dark mode.
  - 网站上的演示短片会按页面语言播放（中文网站播放中文版），并跟随网站的浅色或深色模式。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.8.3, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version changes nothing in the database. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).

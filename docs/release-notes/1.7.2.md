## BioManager 1.7.1

1.7.0 was never published: its server image didn't build. This is the same release, with that fixed.

### New

- **Work in the animal facility without a signal.** The Android app and the desktop app keep a copy of what you need there: the colony's racks, cages, mice, litters and recent weights, your to-dos for two weeks, vials and tanks. When the lab's server can't be reached they show offline screens instead of an error: find a cage by its ID or card, or scan it, and **Record a weight**, **Died**, **Move**, a **Note**, **Litter born**, **Genotyping** (the pups appear at once, ready to weigh), **Wean**, tick a to-do, flip or discard a vial. Each change waits on the device and reaches the lab in order, as you, once you're back in signal. Press and hold **Scan** for **Get ready for the facility** before you go down. The user guide's *Phones and cage cards* has the details.
  - 在没有信号的动物房里也能工作。Android 应用和桌面版会保留一份你在那里需要的内容：鼠群的笼架、笼、小鼠、窝和近期体重，两周内的待办，果蝇管和鱼缸。连不上实验室服务器时，它们显示离线界面而不是报错：按笼号或笼卡查找、或扫描笼卡，然后 **记录体重**、**死亡**、**移动**、**备注**、**产仔**、**基因型鉴定**（幼崽马上出现，可以直接称重）、**断奶**、勾掉待办、给管子转管或丢弃。每项更改都存在设备上，回到有信号的地方就按顺序、以你的身份送达实验室。下去之前长按 **扫描**，选 **Get ready for the facility**。
- **Needs your decision.** If someone changed the same thing while you were offline (set the mouse to Breeder while you marked it dead), your change waits in the account menu under **Needs your decision**, showing what you saw, what is there now and who changed it: **Keep mine**, **Keep theirs** or **Edit**. A different field changed meanwhile is simply kept along with yours, and a change the lab refuses is listed with the reason.
  - 需要你决定：离线期间有人改了同一处时，你的更改会在账户菜单的 **需要你决定** 里等着，显示你看到的、现在的内容和修改的人，供你选择 **保留我的**、**保留他们的** 或 **编辑**。别人改的是另一个字段，就和你的一起保留；实验室拒绝的更改会列出原因。
- **Your devices.** Every computer, phone and browser you're signed in on is listed under **Settings → Devices & copies → Your devices**. Rename one, or **Sign out** a lost phone without changing your password. Admins see **Everyone's devices**.
  - 你的设备：你登录过的每台电脑、手机和浏览器都列在 **设置 → 设备与副本 → 你的设备** 里。可以重命名，或让丢失的手机 **退出登录** 而不用改密码。管理员能看到 **所有人的设备**。
- **Backups only your lab can read.** The lab now has its own key. The server's nightly backups, **Save the whole lab** files and the copies on your computers are locked with it, so a backup that leaks is unreadable. Admins keep its recovery key: **Settings → Your data → Lab key → Show the recovery key**. Write it down; it's the one way to open a backup on a new server.
  - 只有你的实验室能读的备份：实验室现在有自己的密钥。服务器每晚的备份、**保存整个实验室** 的文件和你电脑上的副本都用它加密，泄露的备份无法读取。管理员保管恢复密钥：**设置 → 你的数据 → 实验室密钥 → 显示恢复密钥**。请写下来，它是在新服务器上打开备份的唯一办法。

### Works differently

- **Open your lab joins the lab as one of your devices.** The desktop app asks you to sign in (or to approve a code on a device already signed in), then works on the lab through the app itself rather than opening the server's page, and keeps its daily copy. **Go → Your Lab's BioManager** and **Go → This Computer's BioManager** switch between the two. A desktop that opened a server's page before asks to join it the first time.
  - **打开你的实验室** 现在会把这台电脑作为你的一台设备加入实验室：先登录（或在已登录的设备上批准一个代码），之后桌面版通过应用本身使用实验室，而不是直接打开服务器的页面，并保留每天的副本。**Go → Your Lab's BioManager** 和 **Go → This Computer's BioManager** 在两者之间切换。以前直接打开服务器页面的桌面版，第一次会请你加入。
- **Litter born, Genotyping and Wean are refused on a cage that isn't a Breeding cage**, by the server as well as the page, so an assistant or the API can't record a litter on one either.
  - 不是繁殖笼的笼子，服务器也会拒绝 **产仔**、**基因型鉴定** 和 **断奶**，助手和 API 同样不能在上面记录。
- **Weights are in History**, with undo, like any other record.
  - 体重记录现在进入历史，可以撤销，和其他记录一样。
- **The API's mouse edit can refuse to overwrite**: send back the `version` a mouse was read with, and a mouse changed meanwhile answers 409.
  - API 修改小鼠时可以拒绝覆盖：把读取时的 `version` 一起发回，期间被改过的小鼠会返回 409。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.7.1, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app, and a copy is taken before the database is upgraded. If its window opened your lab server, it asks you to sign in once to join.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first. Then an admin should open **Settings → Your data → Lab key** and keep the recovery key.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build` (the backup service gains the tool that locks backups, and a volume for the lab's key).
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  The database upgrades itself on start: it gains devices, the lab's key, and what offline work needs (a version on the records a phone keeps, changes taken in, and changes held for a decision). Nothing already there changes, and every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Backups taken from now on end in `.age`; older ones restore as before. Restoring on a new machine needs the recovery key: see *The server is lost* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).

## BioManager 1.6.0

### New

- **The BioManager plugin for AI assistants.** Add it once to Claude, Codex, VS Code or Qwen Code (from `gaspolymerase/biomanager`), and "I want to change something in BioManager" is all you need to say: the assistant shows what's due today and asks what you did. **Help → Connect an AI assistant** has the steps for each app, and the user guide has a new page, *Connect an AI assistant*, for Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and the assistants used in China (Cherry Studio, QwenWork, Trae, Doubao, Qwen Code, Kimi Code).
  - AI 助手的 BioManager 插件：在 Claude、Codex、VS Code 或 Qwen Code 里添加一次（来源 `gaspolymerase/biomanager`），以后只说“我想在 BioManager 里改点东西”就行。**帮助 → 连接 AI 助手** 有每个应用的步骤，用户指南新增了“连接 AI 助手”一页。
- **One click for Cursor, VS Code and Cherry Studio.** **Add to Cursor**, **Add to VS Code** and **Add to Cherry Studio** on the Connect page add your BioManager with its address filled in; then you sign in in the browser.
  - 连接页面上的 **添加到 Cursor**、**添加到 VS Code**、**添加到 Cherry Studio**，一键添加你的 BioManager，地址已填好，然后在浏览器里登录。
- **Captures: write it down now, record it later.** At the rack, **Captures → New capture** (or **Capture** in an experiment's **Bench mode**) keeps a note, a dictation, a photo or an instrument's CSV, with the cage or tank you scanned. Later, tell your assistant "process my captures": it proposes the records, and each change says which capture it came from.
  - 速记：在鼠架前用 **速记 → 新速记**（或实验 **实验台模式** 里的 **速记**）记下文字、口述、照片或仪器导出的 CSV；之后对助手说“处理我的速记”，它会提出记录，每处修改都写明来自哪条速记。
- **Every proposed change shows where it came from**: *From your notes: "…"*, or the capture and its photo. **Send back** beside **Approve** and **Discard** returns a proposal with a line saying what's wrong; the assistant reads it and proposes again, corrected.
  - 每处提议的修改都写明依据：“来自你的笔记：……”，或者那条速记和照片。**批准**、**丢弃** 旁边新增 **退回**：写一句哪里不对，助手会改正后重新提议。
- **Assistants reach the whole lab.** Besides animals, experiments and the daily log, they can propose inventory items, amounts used, orders received into stock, samples from an animal; plasmids, glycerol stocks and primers; new notebook pages (marked as drafted, never signed) and sections; calendar events and instrument bookings.
  - 助手现在能覆盖整个实验室：除了动物、实验和每日记录，还能提议库存物品、用量、订单到货入库、从动物取的样本；质粒、甘油菌和引物；新的实验记录页（标明起草，从不签名）；日历事件和仪器预约。
- **Planning with BioManager's own numbers.** An assistant asks BioManager, which works things out from your lab's records: which animals are free by genotype, sex and age; a breeding plan (matings to set up, and by when); animals per group; random groups balanced for sex and litter; when stock runs out; the week's checks; and an experiment's facts for its methods, with the ARRIVE 2.0 checklist. It can also search your records and notebook, and read a record's history and family tree.
  - 用 BioManager 自己算出的数字做计划：可用的动物、繁殖计划、每组需要多少只、按性别和窝别平衡的随机分组、库存何时用完、每周检查，以及实验的方法事实和 ARRIVE 2.0 清单。它还能搜索记录和实验记录本，读取记录的历史和谱系。
- **Planned experiments.** An experiment an assistant designs with you arrives as **Planned**: nothing is due until you press **Start**, which keeps a copy of the plan (groups, **Main readout day**, **Planned test**). After that, changing the main readout, the groups or the test, or leaving an animal out (**Leave out…**), asks for a one-line reason, and **Changes from the plan** lists them beside **Planned → done**. The export gets a sheet of them.
  - 计划中的实验：助手和你一起设计的实验以 **计划中** 出现，按 **开始** 之前没有任何待办；开始时保存计划副本。之后修改主要观测、分组、检验或 **排除…** 动物都会要求写一行原因，**与计划的不同** 列出每一处，旁边是 **计划 → 实际**。

### Works differently

- **ChatGPT keeps its connection**: BioManager now offers the sign-in ChatGPT needs to stay connected, and the Connect page shows where ChatGPT now adds your own servers (Plugins → + → Add custom MCP server, on the web).
  - ChatGPT 能保持连接了；连接页面也写明了 ChatGPT 现在添加自己服务器的位置（网页版 Plugins → + → Add custom MCP server）。
- **A member's lab copy leaves out other people's personal to-dos and events.**
  - 成员导出的实验室副本不再包含别人的个人待办和日程。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.6.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app, and a copy is taken before the database is upgraded.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates → Update now**. It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  The database upgrades itself on start: it gains the captures, a proposal's reason and captures, and an experiment's plan, its changes from the plan and the animals left out. Nothing already there changes, and every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).

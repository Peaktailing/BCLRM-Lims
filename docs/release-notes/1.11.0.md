## BioManager 1.11.0

### New

- **Plate layouts: a plate reader's or qPCR machine's export becomes each animal's readout.** On an experiment, **Plate layout** (in the sheet's toolbar) opens a plate of 24, 48, 96 or 384 wells: put each animal in its wells (several wells are its replicates), with **Blank**, **Standard** and empty wells, or **Fill in order**. **Save layout** keeps it for the next plate. **Read a plate →** takes the export, pasted or as a CSV, TSV or Excel file: an 8 × 12 grid or a list of wells, with several genes for qPCR. Before anything is saved you see each well's value on the plate, replicates far from the others, and wells past what the instrument reads. Then each animal gets its mean, less the blank, read off a standard curve, or as ΔCt, ΔΔCt or fold change. **Save readings** writes them for the day you choose, and the plate's file is kept with the experiment under **Plates read**. An AI assistant can do the same and propose the readings for you to approve.
  - **孔板布局：酶标仪或 qPCR 仪的导出文件直接变成每只动物的观测值。** 在实验里，表格工具栏上的 **孔板布局** 会打开 24、48、96 或 384 孔的板：把每只动物放进它的孔里（几个孔就是它的重复），再标出 **空白**、**标准品** 和空孔，或者用 **按顺序填充**。**保存布局** 会留着它给下一块板用。**读取孔板 →** 接收导出文件，可以粘贴，也可以上传 CSV、TSV 或 Excel：8 × 12 的网格或按孔列出的清单都行，qPCR 可以有多个基因。保存之前，你会在板上看到每个孔的数值、和其他重复差得远的孔，以及超出仪器读数范围的孔。然后每只动物得到它的平均值，可以减去空白、按标准曲线换算，或者算成 ΔCt、ΔΔCt 或倍数变化。**保存读数** 会写进你选的那一天，板的文件和实验保存在一起，列在 **已读的孔板** 下。AI 助手也能做同样的事，并把读数作为提议交给你批准。

### Works differently

- **Every injection keeps the lot of the reagent it used, however it is recorded:** on the experiment page, **Record manipulation**, bench mode, or an AI assistant. It is the step's planned bottle unless you choose another, or the one bottle in the inventory named like the agent. A different **Lot** picks the bottle with that lot. A lot already expired that day is recorded with a warning. Tick **Take what they got off the inventory** to take the animals' amounts off the bottle's quantity; **Undo: not done** puts them back.
  - **每次注射都会记下所用试剂的批号，不管在哪里记录：** 实验页面、**记录处理**、实验台模式，或 AI 助手。默认是这一步计划用的那瓶；你可以另选，否则就用库存里和药物同名的那一瓶。填另一个 **批号** 会换成那个批号的那瓶。当天已经过期的批号会照样记录，但会给出提醒。勾选 **从库存中扣除给药量**，会把动物们用的量从那瓶的数量里扣掉；**撤销：未完成** 会把它放回去。

### Fixed

- Deleting an experiment from the colony's list also removes its readings; on a lab server it could fail.
  - 从鼠群列表删除实验时，会把它的读数一起删除；以前在实验室服务器上可能删除失败。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.11.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  The database upgrades itself on start: it gains an experiment's plate layouts and the plates read onto them, both empty. Nothing already there changes, and every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).

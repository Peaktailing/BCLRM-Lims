## BioManager 1.14.2

### New

- **Set field offers Pack size.** Tick items in an inventory that keeps stock, choose **Set field → Pack size**, and type what one of their unit holds (`10 mL`): every ticked item gets it at once, so an amount used in mL comes off as part of an ampoule or vial.
  - **设置字段里可以选包装规格。** 在库存表格里勾选物品，选 **设置字段 → 包装规格**，填上一个单位装多少（`10 mL`），所勾选的物品一次全部填好，之后按 mL 用掉的量会换算成支或瓶的一部分。

### Works differently

- **Not weighed in their experiment covers every animal weighed in an experiment,** not only mice: an animal of any other organism in an active experiment whose readout is body weight is listed on Home's **Animals that may need a look**, and counted in the morning notification, when its experiment has no weight for it for longer than the regimen allows.
  - **实验中未按时称重现在涵盖实验里称重的所有动物**，不只是小鼠：观测指标为体重、正在进行的实验里，其他生物的动物超过处理方案允许的天数没有体重记录时，也会列在首页的 **可能需要查看的动物** 里，并计入早上的通知。

### Fixed

- **A picture added in the notebook while someone else has the page open shows for them at once,** not as a broken picture until the page saves.
  - **有人同时打开实验记录本页面时，新加的图片会立刻显示给他们**，不再在保存之前显示为损坏的图片。
- **One of an item is written in the singular:** an AI assistant's proposal says *1 ampoule used*, and takes *1 ampoule* for an item kept in ampoules.
  - **用量为一时用单数：** AI 助手的提议写作 *1 ampoule used*，对按 ampoules 记录的物品也接受 *1 ampoule*。
- **An AI assistant no longer offers an admin other people's mice as theirs to use** when it looks for animals for an experiment. An admin may still edit them.
  - **AI 助手为实验找动物时，不再把别人的小鼠当作管理员自己可用的小鼠。** 管理员仍然可以编辑它们。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.14.2, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  This version changes nothing in the database's tables. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).

## BioManager 1.10.0

### New

- **Strain, a column of its own for each mouse.** It sits after **Custom tag** in the mouse sheet, and is in the mouse's dialog too: the mouse's background, such as C57BL/6J or BALB/cJ. As you type it offers C57BL/6J, C57BL/6N, BALB/cJ, 129S1/SvImJ, FVB/NJ, DBA/2J, C3H/HeJ, NOD/ShiLtJ, CD-1 and Mixed, and you can type one of your own. Change the list on the colony's **Dropdowns** tab (Column: *strain*). **Add many** sets one strain for the whole batch, and search finds a mouse by its strain. Every mouse starts with none.
  - **每只小鼠都有了单独的“品系”列。** 它在小鼠表格里 **自定义标识** 的后面，小鼠的详情窗口里也有：记录小鼠的遗传背景，例如 C57BL/6J 或 BALB/cJ。输入时会列出 C57BL/6J、C57BL/6N、BALB/cJ、129S1/SvImJ、FVB/NJ、DBA/2J、C3H/HeJ、NOD/ShiLtJ、CD-1 和 Mixed，也可以输入你们自己的。在鼠群的 **下拉选项** 标签页里修改这个列表（列选 *strain*）。**批量添加** 可以给整批小鼠设同一个品系，搜索也能按品系找到小鼠。所有小鼠一开始都没有品系。

### Works differently

- **Importing mice, a column called "Strain" or "Background" goes into Strain.** It used to go into the genotype. If your sheet's "Strain" column holds lines such as Ai14 or PV-Cre, point it at **Genotype** on the page that matches the columns.
  - **导入小鼠时，名为“Strain”或“Background”的列会进入品系。** 以前它会进入基因型。如果你们表格里的“Strain”列写的是 Ai14、PV-Cre 这样的品系系谱，请在匹配列的页面把它指向 **基因型**。
- **The mouse sheet's columns start as they come.** The new column moved the ones after it, so a choice of hidden columns made under **Columns** on the mouse sheet starts afresh; untick them again once.
  - **小鼠表格的列恢复默认显示。** 新的一列让后面的列都移了位，所以以前在 **列** 里隐藏的列会重新显示；再取消勾选一次即可。

### Fixed

- **The ••• menu in a sheet's toolbar opens over everything.** On a short sheet the bar with the count and pages covered **Import from Excel**, and in a narrow window the sidebar covered the menu.
  - **表格工具栏的 ••• 菜单会显示在最上层。** 以前表格很短时，显示数量和页码的那一栏会挡住 **从 Excel 导入**，窗口较窄时侧边栏也会挡住菜单。

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.10.0, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server on 1.2.6 or later**: **Settings → Version → Update now** (before 1.7.5, **Settings → Devices & copies → Updates**). It backs up first.
- **Lab server on an earlier version**, by hand:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.
  4. Run `sudo host/install.sh`, which sets up **Update now** for next time.

  The database upgrades itself on start: each mouse gains an empty Strain, and a lab with no strain choices yet gets the common ones. Nothing already there changes, and every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
